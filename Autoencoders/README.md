Experiment 7 – Autoencoders and Variational Autoencoders
End-to-End Study of Autoencoders, Convolutional Autoencoders, Denoising Autoencoders and Variational Autoencoders

This experiment provides an end-to-end study of different types of autoencoders for image representation, reconstruction, denoising, and generative modeling.

The experiment uses the MNIST Handwritten Digit Dataset and implements four major models:

Fully Connected Autoencoder
Convolutional Autoencoder (CAE)
Denoising Convolutional Autoencoder
Variational Autoencoder (VAE)

Objective

The objective of this experiment is to develop an understanding of autoencoders and their variants for:

Image representation
Image reconstruction
Image denoising
Latent-space visualization
Generative modeling
Comparison of deterministic and probabilistic latent representations

The same MNIST dataset and controlled experimental setup are used throughout the experiment.

Analysis

The experiment includes:

Original vs reconstructed images
Training vs validation reconstruction loss
Reconstruction MSE
Reconstruction MAE
Mean SSIM
